Frequently Asked Questions

How can I confirm receipt of my electronic transfer?

Please include your Name and Offering Category (e.g., "John Doe - Tithe") in the transfer narration, or send a proof of transfer to +234 (0) 803 123 4567 (WhatsApp) for provincial acknowledgement.

Can I give from abroad (Diaspora partners)?

Yes! For international wire transfers (USD / GBP / EUR), kindly contact the Provincial Secretariat at finance@rccgprovince5minna.org for our corresponding domiciliary SWIFT details.

Is my donation secure?

All accounts listed are direct corporate institutional accounts registered under The Redeemed Christian Church of God with Nigerian licensed commercial banks.
