Welcome Message

Welcome to RCCG Niger Province 5

The Redeemed Christian Church of God, Niger Province 5, headquartered in the historic capital city of Minna, Niger State, is an apostolic family charged with planting the kingdom of God across every community, campus, and household.

Under the spiritual leadership of our father-in-the-Lord, the General Overseer Pastor E.A. Adeboye, and our visionary Provincial PastorPastor Oluyemi Oluwasegun, our mission remains unflinching: to make heaven, take as many people with us, live holistically Christlike lives, and plant churches within 5 minutes reach.
